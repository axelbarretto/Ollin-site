Is this a fit?

Good fit

  • ✓ Your site hasn't been touched in years, and it shows.
  • ✓ You're a little embarrassed to send people to it.
  • ✓ You know something's off, but can't pin down what.
  • ✓ You want a site you can actually edit yourself later.

Not a fit

  • ✕ You need a complex e-commerce build with heavy inventory — different tool, happy to point you elsewhere.
  • ✕ You just want a logo tweak — that's a designer, not a web rebuild.
  • ✕ You're not ready to make any decisions yet — that's fine, reach out when you are.

Questions

Do I need a full rebuild or just a fix?

Depends on what's actually wrong — that's what the first conversation is for. Some sites need to start over, most just need the right things fixed.

How long does it take?

A targeted fix can be days. A full rebuild is usually a few weeks, depending on scope. You'll get a timeline before anything starts.

Will I own the site afterward?

Yes. Your domain, your hosting, your login. No lock-in, no "call us to change a sentence."

What if I already like my site, just not the results?

Then it's a fix, not a rebuild — I'm not going to tear down something that's working just to sell you a bigger project.
